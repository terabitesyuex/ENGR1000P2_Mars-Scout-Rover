- ROS2/RViz is external diagnostic evidence. It does not add ROS2 as a baseline runtime dependency and does not replace the repository recording, replay, or visualization path.

## Data-Quality Boundary

The locked captures include 41 points with quality 0 and 132 points above 12,000 mm, with a maximum recorded distance of 16,360 mm. They are retained as raw evidence rather than silently filtered. This means the evidence verifies physical acquisition and visible geometric response, but not clean-packet rate, production filtering, or manufacturer-grade range behavior.

## Still UNVERIFIED

- Vendor-tool device information, health state, scan mode, and measured scan frequency.
- Exact serial identifier and hardware revision.
- Wall-clock scan timing, corrupt-node rate, dropped-sample rate, and long-duration stability.
- Controlled reference geometry, uncertainty, absolute range accuracy, angular calibration, and repeatability.
- Final rover mounting position, yaw, and `base_link` transform.
- STM32/OpenRF1 transport, ESP32/WiFi transport, and full-rover operation.

Repository automation validates only committed files and opens no serial port, accesses no hardware, and flashes nothing.
